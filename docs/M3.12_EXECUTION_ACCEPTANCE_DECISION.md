## Plane separation preserved

The Programming Control Plane, media-processing workers, Playout &
Automation Runtime, Source Encoder Layer, and Listener-Facing Icecast Layer
remained strictly decoupled and separately deployable throughout. PostgreSQL
remains the sole future live persistence store; Icecast 2.x remains the sole
future listener-facing platform. Neither was touched.

## What this acceptance does not authorize

This is Phase 2 execution acceptance only. It does not itself grant M4, M5,
M7, M9, M10, or production authority, and it does not repeat or extend the
rehearsal. The separate `m3_operational_acceptance_authority` decision is
recorded next.
