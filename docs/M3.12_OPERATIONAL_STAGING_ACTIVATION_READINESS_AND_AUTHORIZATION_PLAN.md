# M3.12 — Operational Staging Activation Authorization and Isolated Media-Lifecycle Rehearsal

## Status, purpose, and non-authority

**Status: approved two-phase exception; readiness plan documented but not
accepted; isolated rehearsal unstarted.** M3.12 narrowly reopens the completed
M3 sequence after M3.11. M3.1–M3.11 remain complete and accepted in their
local-only, non-operational scope. M3.12 is the sole approved exception path
for a readiness/authorization plan and, only after explicit human approvals,
an isolated non-production media-lifecycle rehearsal.

This is the authoritative M3.12 record in [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md#m312--operational-staging-activation-authorization-and-isolated-media-lifecycle-rehearsal).
It does not itself accept the readiness plan or authorize an operational
action. It creates no standing authority, target, cohort, credential, secret
reference, path, address, service, or execution instruction.

## Strictly ordered phases

### Phase 1 — Non-executable readiness and authorization plan

Phase 1 defines only content-free classifications, named authorities,
one-action approval gates, stop/no-go conditions, recovery ownership,
rollback-versus-forward-fix decisions, and evidence requirements. Its staging
target must be classified as `isolated_staging`, `non_production`, and either
`empty` or `safely_classified`. The latter is a bounded safe-input decision,
not an identity or permission to access input. Its future cohort is limited to
the `minimal_rehearsal_cohort` classification; no real media is identified,
selected, accessed, copied, named, or imported by this plan.

Phase 1 must be separately accepted before Phase 2 may be proposed. Acceptance
of the plan is not approval of any Phase 2 action.

### Phase 2 — Separately approved isolated operational rehearsal

Phase 2 may occur only after Phase 1 is accepted and the separate authority
for every action to be taken has granted a one-action approval. Those approvals
must name only the bounded action class, reviewed artifact identity, target
classification, authority category, change window, content-free evidence
boundary, stop conditions, and decision owner. They must never contain a media
identity, protected value, credential, address, path, URL, token, CSRF value,
or raw SQL. Completion of one action cannot authorize another action, a retry,
or broader scope.

At a high level only, the separately approved rehearsal may validate a safely
classified non-production staging target and a minimal explicitly approved
cohort through controlled M3 lifecycle categories: intake, quarantine,
processing/analysis, review, cleanup, recovery/restore validation, and evidence
review. It must verify station isolation and content-free audit/evidence at
each approved boundary. This record supplies no command, target, media cohort,
or adapter to perform any of those categories.

## M3.12 execution acceptance and stop boundary

M3.12 execution can be complete and accepted only when all separately approved
actions have produced the required content-free evidence and an authorized
acceptance decision confirms all of the following:

- the target remained isolated, non-production, and empty or safely classified;
- the cohort remained minimal and within every explicit action approval;
- every entity, reference, lookup, fixture, outcome, and audit record used
  `id + station_id`, with `not_found` for missing/out-of-scope resources and
  `station_reference_forbidden` for internal cross-station mismatches;
- lifecycle, cleanup, recovery/restore-validation, and evidence-review
  outcomes met their separately approved bounded criteria;
- audit and evidence remained content-free; and
- no prohibited plane, listener, production, or wider operational action was
  activated.

It is a no-go and the action must stop if the target is ambiguous or not
isolated/non-production and empty or safely classified; the cohort is not
minimal; a required approval is missing; an authority is shared or overbroad;
station scope is ambiguous; evidence cannot remain content-free; a protected
value would be exposed; or any prohibited plane/action would be activated. The
named recovery authority, not automation, chooses rollback, deferral, or a
forward-fix under a new separately approved bounded action. No automatic retry,
rollback, recovery, credential substitution, or privilege expansion is
permitted.

The Programming Control Plane, media-processing workers, Playout & Automation
Runtime, Source Encoder Layer, and Listener-Facing Icecast Layer remain
strictly decoupled and separately deployable throughout both phases.

## Exclusions and preserved constraints

Until the relevant Phase 2 action has its separately accepted one-action
approval, M3.12 authorizes no media selection, access, copy, import, ingestion,
processing, analysis, normalization, metadata-provider activity, quarantine,
cleanup, PostgreSQL connection, migration execution, container, worker,
subprocess, FFmpeg, parser, media tooling, network call, provider call, or
filesystem operation. This documentation change performs none of them.

M3.12 never authorizes production action, listener impact, scheduling,
publication, playout, DSP, runtime, encoding, relay, Icecast, infrastructure,
deployment, DNS change, legacy-system change, or M10 appliance work. PostgreSQL
remains the sole future live persistence store; SQLite is prohibited. Icecast
2.x remains the sole future listener-facing streaming platform. Windows, SAM,
PAL, legacy SHOUTcast, and arbitrary process control are not introduced.

Audit and evidence remain content-free and must never contain media contents,
titles, filenames, paths, URLs, metadata payloads, credentials, tokens, CSRF
values, or raw SQL. No dashboard or API action may implicitly trigger an
operational action.

## Revised sequence and next milestone

M3.12 is a narrowly bounded post-acceptance exception, not a revocation of M3
acceptance. The revised sequence is **accepted M3.1–M3.11 → accepted Phase 1
M3.12 readiness plan → separately approved and accepted Phase 2 M3.12 isolated
rehearsal → M4.1**. The next unstarted roadmap milestone is **M3.12 Phase 1
acceptance**, followed only by separately approved Phase 2 execution. **M4.1 —
Schedule-domain and versioning contract** remains deferred until M3.12
execution is completed and accepted.
