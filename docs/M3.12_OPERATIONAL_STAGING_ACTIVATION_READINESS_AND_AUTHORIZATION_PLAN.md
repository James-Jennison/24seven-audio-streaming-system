# M3.12 — Operational Staging Activation Readiness and Authorization Plan

## Status, purpose, and non-authority

**Status: future post-acceptance exception; planning only; non-executable and
non-operational.** M3.12 narrowly reopens the completed M3 sequence after
M3.11 to define an authorization framework for the first isolated,
non-production media-lifecycle rehearsal. It preserves M3.1–M3.11 as complete
and accepted in their local-only, non-operational scope; it neither revokes
that acceptance nor authorizes a staging action.

This is the authoritative planning record for [M3.12 in the
roadmap](ROADMAP.md#m312--operational-staging-activation-readiness-and-authorization-plan).
M3.12 must be completed and separately accepted before M4.1 may begin. Its
completion still does not authorize staging execution: every future rehearsal
gate listed below requires its own explicit human approval.

## Future authorization framework

M3.12 may define only content-free classifications, approval records, and
future acceptance criteria. It may not select or identify a target, media
cohort, credential, secret reference, path, address, or service.

| Future gate                        | Required bounded decision                                                           | Not an authorization for                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Staging-target validation          | Confirm an isolated, non-production, empty or otherwise safely classified target.   | Connection, migration, infrastructure change, or service start.               |
| Media-access approval              | Confirm a minimal rehearsal-cohort classification and named authority.              | Selecting, naming, copying, accessing, or importing real media.               |
| Intake-execution approval          | Confirm reviewed lifecycle artifact, station scope, input boundary, and stop owner. | Starting intake, ingestion, or persistence activity.                          |
| Processing/analysis approval       | Confirm a bounded worker/analysis authority and disabled downstream planes.         | Starting workers, media tools, normalization, cue/fade, or provider activity. |
| Review approval                    | Confirm safe lifecycle, quarantine, and metadata-review categories.                 | Reviewing media contents, resolving metadata, or changing a runtime setting.  |
| Cleanup and recovery approval      | Confirm cleanup, rollback/forward-fix authority, and recovery owner.                | Cleanup, backup, restore, rollback, or recovery execution.                    |
| Evidence-review approval           | Confirm content-free evidence categories and decision owner.                        | Reading protected data or emitting content-bearing diagnostics.               |
| M3 operational-acceptance approval | Confirm all prior approved bounded actions have required evidence.                  | M4, M5, M7, M9, M10, or production authority.                                 |

The future plan sequence may describe controlled import, quarantine,
metadata-handling, loudness/normalization analysis, cue/fade analysis, review,
lifecycle evidence, cleanup, rollback/recovery, and acceptance only as
approval-gated categories. No sequence step is executable from this record.

## Future acceptance criteria and stop boundary

Before a separately proposed M3 rehearsal can be considered, its proposal must
define all of the following without protected values or operational commands:

- isolated staging-target and minimal-cohort classifications;
- separate human approvals for every gate in this record;
- `id + station_id` scope for every future entity, reference, lookup, fixture,
  outcome, and audit record, with `not_found` for missing/out-of-scope records
  and `station_reference_forbidden` for internal cross-station references;
- content-free evidence categories for authority, target classification,
  lifecycle category, station-isolation result, downstream-plane inactivity,
  stop, cleanup, recovery, and acceptance outcomes;
- explicit stop conditions for target ambiguity, unexpected input, scope change,
  missing/overbroad authority, evidence-redaction failure, cross-station
  mismatch, or any plane activation; and
- named decision ownership for rollback versus separately approved forward-fix,
  with no automatic retry, recovery, or privilege expansion.

The Programming Control Plane, media-processing workers, Playout & Automation
Runtime, Source Encoder Layer, and Listener-Facing Icecast Layer remain
strictly decoupled and separately deployable. M4 scheduling, M5 runtime/DSP,
M7 encoding/Icecast, M9 cutover, and M10 appliance work remain inactive and
separately gated.

## Explicit exclusions and preserved constraints

M3.12 authorizes none of the following: real-media selection, access, copy,
import, ingestion, processing, analysis, normalization, metadata-provider
activity, quarantine, cleanup, PostgreSQL connection, migration execution,
containers, workers, subprocesses, FFmpeg, parsers, media tooling, scheduling,
publication, playout, runtime/DSP, encoding, relay, Icecast, infrastructure,
deployment, DNS, listener impact, or legacy-system changes.

PostgreSQL remains the sole future live persistence store; SQLite is prohibited.
Icecast 2.x remains the sole future listener-facing streaming platform. Audit
and evidence remain content-free and must never contain media contents, titles,
filenames, paths, URLs, metadata payloads, credentials, tokens, CSRF values, or
raw SQL. No dashboard or API action may implicitly trigger an operational
action. Windows, SAM, PAL, legacy SHOUTcast, and arbitrary process control are
not introduced.

## Revised sequence and next human gate

M3.12 is a narrowly bounded post-acceptance exception, not a revocation of M3
acceptance. The revised sequence is **accepted M3.1–M3.11 → M3.12 planning and
separate acceptance → M4.1**. The next unstarted milestone is **M3.12**;
M4.1 remains deferred until M3.12 is completed and separately accepted.
